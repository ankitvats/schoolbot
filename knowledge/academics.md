# Academics

## Subjects Offered

For Classes 1-5 (Primary): English, Hindi, Mathematics, Environmental Science (EVS), Computer Science, Art, Music, and Physical Education.

For Classes 6-8 (Middle): English, Hindi, Mathematics, Science, Social Science, Computer Science, Sanskrit (optional), Art, Music, and Physical Education.

For Class 9 and Class 10 (Secondary): English, Hindi, Mathematics, Science, Social Science, and one optional subject (Computer Science / Physical Education / Sanskrit). Subjects in class 9 include English, Hindi, Mathematics, Science, and Social Science.

For Classes 11-12 (Senior Secondary), the following streams are offered:

- **PCM (Science)**: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, and optional (Computer Science/Physical Education)
- **PCB (Science)**: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, and optional (Computer Science/Physical Education)
- **Commerce Stream**: Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, English, and optional (Mathematics/Informatics Practices)

## Teaching Pedagogy

G.A.V. International School follows a unique pedagogy that implements a curriculum for **Physical, Mental, Emotional, Intellectual, and Spiritual progress** for achieving multifaceted development. The school uses **activity-based learning** methods. The prime focus is on Holistic Development which includes both Scholastic and Co-scholastic activities.

## Examination Pattern

The academic year follows the CBSE examination pattern with continuous assessment:

- Unit Test 1: July
- Mid-Term Examination: September
- Unit Test 2: November
- Final Examination: February-March

Internal assessment includes project work, assignments, class participation, and periodic tests.

## Grading System

The school follows the CBSE grading system with grades from A1 to E. Internal assessment includes project work, assignments, class participation, and periodic tests.

## Board Exam Results

**CBSE Board Results (2024):**

- **Class 10**: 42 students appeared, 35 passed — **81% pass rate**
- **Class 12**: 22 students appeared, 19 passed — **82% pass rate**

For board exam results (Class 10 and 12), students can check results on the CBSE official website.

## Homework Policy

Homework is assigned daily to reinforce classroom learning. Students are expected to complete homework on time. A homework diary must be maintained and signed by parents daily.

## General School Timetable

**General School Timetable:**

- School hours: 8:00 AM – 2:30 PM (Mon-Fri), 8:00 AM – 12:30 PM (Saturday)
- 8 periods per day (40 minutes each)
- Morning break: 10:00-10:20 AM
- Lunch break: 12:20-12:50 PM
- Assembly: 7:50-8:00 AM daily

Subject-wise timetables vary by class. Contact your class teacher for the detailed weekly schedule.
