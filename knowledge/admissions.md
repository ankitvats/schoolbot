# Admissions

## Admission Process

The admission process at G.A.V. International School involves the following steps:

1. Fill out the **online application form** on the school website or through Ezyschooling
2. Pay the **application fee of ₹5,000**
3. Submit required documents
4. Entrance test / interaction (for applicable classes)
5. Parent-student interaction with the Principal
6. Admission confirmation and fee payment

Admissions are **ongoing** throughout the year, with the last date for the next session typically being **December 31**. The academic session runs from **April to March**.

## Classes Available for Admission

Admissions are open for all classes from **Pre-Nursery to Class 12**, including:
Pre-Nursery, Nursery, LKG, UKG, Class 1 through Class 12.

## Documents Required for Admission

The following documents are required for admission:

- Birth Certificate (original + photocopy)
- Previous school Transfer Certificate (TC)
- Report card of last 2 years
- 4 passport-size photographs
- Aadhar card of student and parents
- Address proof
- Medical fitness certificate

For Pre-Nursery and Nursery admissions, only Birth Certificate and Aadhar are needed.

## Admission Age Criteria

Age Criteria (as of March 31):

- Pre-Nursery: 2.5+ years
- Nursery: 3+ years
- LKG: 4+ years
- UKG: 5+ years
- Class 1: 6+ years

For higher classes, age should be appropriate as per CBSE norms.

## Transfer Certificate

Transfer Certificate (TC) facility is available. Parents can apply for TC through the school office or download from the school website.

## School Visit

You can visit the school to meet teachers and explore school facilities. School visit timings are **8:00 AM to 1:30 PM**. Contact the school to schedule a visit.
