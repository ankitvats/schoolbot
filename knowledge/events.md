# Events and Activities

## Extra-Curricular Activities

G.A.V. International School offers a wide range of extra-curricular activities:

- **Creative Arts**: Art and Craft, Dance, Music, Drama
- **Literary Activities**: Debate and Discussion, Creative Writing, Story-Telling, Recitation, Spelling Bee, Calligraphy
- **Competitions**: Quiz, Olympiad, Declamation Contest, Drawing, Handwriting, Wall Magazine Decoration
- **Cultural Activities**: Festival Celebrations, Special Assemblies, Cultural Programs
- **Clubs**: Gardening, Cooking Classes
- **Excursions**: Picnics, Educational Tours, Trips & Excursions

## Annual Events

**Key Annual Events:**

- **Annual Day / Cultural Fest** — Cultural performances, prize distribution, and special guest lectures
- **Annual Sports Day** — Track & field events, inter-house competitions
- **Science Exhibition** — Student projects and science experiments
- **Annual Fun Fair** — Games, food stalls, and entertainment for students and families
- **Christmas Carnival** — Festive celebrations
- **Youth Parliament** — Leadership and civic awareness activities
- **Summer Camp** — Activity-based learning during summer break

## Workshops and Seminars

The school regularly conducts:

- **Career Counselling Sessions** — Guidance for students on career options
- **Parenting Seminars** — Sessions for parents on child development
- **Workshops** — Various skill-building and educational workshops
- **Exhibitions** — Art exhibitions and school exhibitions

## Community Involvement

- **Share for Society** — Social responsibility initiatives
- **Blood Donation Camps** — Community service activities
- **Scouts, Guides & NCC** — Character building and discipline programs

## School Holidays

**Major Holidays 2026-27:**

- Summer Vacation: May 15 – June 30
- Dussehra Break: October 1-5
- Diwali Break: October 20-27
- Winter Break: December 25 – January 1
- Holi: March 14
- Republic Day: January 26
- Independence Day: August 15

Complete holiday list (Annual Calendar / List of Holidays) is shared at the beginning of the academic year. It can also be found in the Downloads section of the school website.
