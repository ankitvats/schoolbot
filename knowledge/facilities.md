# School Facilities

G.A.V. International School, Sector-7 has modern facilities to support holistic development of students on its 1.32-acre campus.

## Smart Classrooms

All classrooms are equipped with **smart boards** and technology-enabled teaching tools. The classrooms are **air-conditioned** and have **WiFi** connectivity. The content in smart classes has been specially created for the Indian curriculum and is based on the philosophy of "Seeing is Believing." By associating new material through smart class with something familiar, students can better understand and retain new information through visuals.

## Computer Lab

The computer lab is equipped with a network of computers. Students are exposed to net-surfing for collecting specialized information, project work, and PowerPoint presentations. Computer education is part of the curriculum from early classes, covering spreadsheets, database management, graphics, internet, and basic programming.

## Science Laboratories

G.A.V. International School has well-equipped Science Laboratories that provide a stimulating environment for students to explore and innovate. Each lab is designed to ensure hands-on learning, encouraging curiosity and critical thinking. Students use the equipment for practical experiments as part of the CBSE curriculum.

## Library

The library serves as part and parcel of the academic setup. It offers a rich stock of knowledge with books related to different subjects and fields, besides newspapers, magazines, and journals. There are separate arrangements for students and teachers. The library is a valuable resource during leisure time and for academic needs.

## Art and Craft Room

The school has a dedicated Art & Craft room. Art stimulates the imagination and emphasizes the development of fine motor skills — the coordination of a child's body and mind. Activities include drawing, painting, clay modelling, paper craft, and other creative activities.

## Sports Facilities

The school has a large playground for outdoor sports and fitness. Sports activities include:

- **Outdoor Games**: Football, Basketball, Cricket, Volleyball, Skating
- **Indoor Games**: Chess, Carom Board, Table Tennis
- **Fitness**: Yoga, Aerobics

Students are encouraged to participate in sports activities for health and fitness. The school has won awards in District Roller Skating Championship — student Jatin Nehru won a Gold Medal and Aayu Pahal won a Silver Medal at Young Leaders Sports Club.

_Note: The school does not have a swimming pool, gym, or horse riding facility._

## School Bus and Transport

School transport facility is available with **GPS-enabled buses** for tracking. The bus tracking app allows parents to track the school bus. Transport is optional and fees vary by route. Contact the school for route details.

## Cafeteria

The school has a cafeteria/canteen facility available on campus for students and staff.

## Medical Room

A medical room is available on campus equipped with first-aid supplies and basic medical equipment. Annual health check-ups are conducted for all students. Emergency contact numbers of parents are maintained.

## Safety and Security

The school has **24x7 CCTV surveillance** across the campus for the safety and security of students. GPS bus tracking is available for transport safety.

## Auditorium

The school has an auditorium/media room for assemblies, events, cultural performances, and special programs.
