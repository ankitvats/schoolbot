# Fees

## Fee Structure

**Approximate Annual Fee Structure (2026-27):**

- Pre-Nursery: ~₹94,700/year
- Nursery – UKG: ~₹1,01,000/year
- Class 1-2: ~₹1,23,500/year
- Class 3-5: ~₹1,28,300/year
- Class 6-8: ~₹1,30,700/year
- Class 9-10: ~₹1,54,500/year
- Class 11-12: ~₹1,62,900/year

The total cost of admission usually starts at approximately ₹94,700 and can go up to ₹1,62,900. This includes Registration Fees, Composite Fees, Exam Fees, and Transport Fees.

**Fee Breakdown (approximate for Class 10):**

- Registration/Application Fee: ₹5,000 (one-time)
- Admission Fee: ₹30,000 (one-time)
- Composite Fee: ~₹6,957/month

**Monthly fee** generally ranges from approximately ₹6,000/month for Nursery to ₹10,000/month for senior classes.

_Note: Fees are approximate and may vary. Please contact the school directly for the latest fee details._

## Fee Discount

A **5% discount on tuition fee** is offered if the entire annual fee is deposited at the time of admission.

## Payment Methods

Fees can be paid through:

- **Online** — Bank transfer / NEFT / UPI
- **Cheque** — In favor of 'G.A.V. International School'
- **Cash** — At the school accounts office

All fees must be deposited on or before the **10th of every month**.

## Transport Fees

Transport facility is available and optional. Transport fees vary based on the distance/route. Contact the school for route-wise transport fee details.
