# General Information

## About the School

Our school is **G.A.V. International School, Sector-7**, located in the heart of Gurugram, Haryana. The school was founded in **1996** and is operated under **Vivek Shiksha Samiti**. The current campus at Sector-7 Extension, Krishna Colony was commissioned in **December 2021** on a **1.32 acres** campus. G.A.V. stands for values rooted in the philosophy of **Gita, Veda, and contemporary education**. We are a **CBSE-affiliated co-educational day school** committed to academic excellence and holistic development, with a student strength of **1,187+** students.

## Vision and Mission

**Vision:** To nurture enlightened, confident, and compassionate global citizens rooted in Indian values and empowered by modern education.

**Mission:** To provide holistic education that blends the eternal wisdom of the Gita and Vedas with modern scientific learning. We strive to create an environment that nurtures intellectual curiosity, moral strength, emotional balance, and creative expression — enabling every child to emerge as a confident, compassionate, and responsible global citizen.

We believe that every child has the potential to reach perfection provided he/she has the right motivation and the right guidance. It is our aim to provide holistic stimulus to each child to maximize every aspect of his or her potential — intellectual, physical, emotional, social, ethical, artistic, or cultural.
