# School Rules and Code of Conduct

## Uniform and Dress Code

All students must wear the prescribed school uniform on all working days. The uniform includes the school-designated shirt, trousers or skirt, school tie, black shoes, and white socks. On Saturdays, students may wear the house color t-shirt with jeans. Hair should be neatly combed. Boys must maintain short hair. Girls with long hair must tie it with black or navy blue ribbons. No jewelry or accessories are allowed except simple studs for girls.

## Attendance Policy

A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory for all students to be eligible to appear in final examinations as per CBSE norms. Leave applications must be submitted in writing to the class teacher. Medical leave of more than 3 days requires a doctor's certificate. Habitual absenteeism without valid reason may lead to disciplinary action. Parents will be notified when a student is absent.

## Mobile Phone Policy

Mobile phones are strictly not allowed inside the school campus. If a student is found with a mobile phone, it will be confiscated and returned only to the parent. Smart watches with calling or internet features are also not permitted. Students needing to contact parents in emergencies can use the school office phone at +91-9625568475.

## Discipline and Behavior

Students are expected to be respectful to teachers, staff, and fellow students at all times. Bullying, physical violence, or use of abusive language will result in strict disciplinary action including suspension. Damage to school property must be compensated by the student's family. Students must maintain cleanliness in classrooms and campus. The school has 24x7 CCTV surveillance for safety.

## Fee Payment Rules

All fees must be deposited on or before the **10th of every month**. A 5% discount on tuition fee is available if the entire annual fee is deposited at the time of admission.

## Examination Rules

All students must appear for unit tests, mid-term, and final examinations as per the schedule. Use of unfair means during examinations will result in cancellation of the exam and disciplinary action. Students arriving late for exams will not be given extra time. Report cards are issued after each term examination.
